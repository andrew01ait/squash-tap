Title: **Keep Score – Racket Sports**

Short description: **Score six racket sports on Wear OS, offline, with optional live sharing.**

## Full description

Keep score on your Wear OS watch for badminton, padel, pickleball, squash, table tennis and tennis.

Tap the rally winner, correct a mistake with Undo, and keep separate results and statistics for each sport. Choose the scoring format you play, including supported singles/doubles modes and optional serving guidance.

- Large watch controls and Classic, Neon Lime or Sunlight scoreboard colours.
- Optional player names, your own name, favourite formats and quick rematch.
- Saved results with player/date/mode filters and Reset all filters.
- Enter completed sets or record a separate past match.
- View results and transfer CSV or complete backups through a temporary QR link in your phone browser.
- Start optional live result sharing so spectators can follow a read-only browser scoreboard. Matching saved players can show head-to-head and the last meeting. Final results remain available for ten minutes.
- Open your current match through a watch Tile or watch-face complication.

Playing and saving scores work offline. Optional browser transfers and live score updates need internet. No account is required.

Keep Score records points and results; it does not make referee decisions or replace tournament rules. It does not measure heart rate, GPS tracks, calories or health outcomes.
